Lines are copied exactly as the game has them, typos included. *Italics in brackets* are what happens between lines. Indented notes under a line are things to look at (37 of them).

To change a line, quote it and give the new wording, or edit this file and tell me. I'll put the changes into the game.

Not included: on-screen hints, buttons and banners. Those are in GAME\_TEXT.md next to this file.
